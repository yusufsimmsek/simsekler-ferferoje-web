# UX Standard

## Purpose

ArchiFlow workflows must make architectural-office work easier, safer, and more trustworthy.

## Requirements

- Users must always understand where they are, what is next, and why an action is blocked.
- Preserve the 8.3 urban transformation workflow from project creation to final offer package.
- Keep human approval points explicit for legal, financial, or irreversible decisions.
- Use domain-specific Turkish language that architects and office staff understand.
- Expose source, calculation, review, and approval status for trust-critical outputs.

## Review Gate

Product Manager and UX Researcher must approve changed critical paths. QA Lead must verify failure and blocked states.
