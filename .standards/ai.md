# AI Standard

## Purpose

AI in ArchiFlow must automate labor while preserving traceability, safety, and professional accountability.

## Requirements

- Prefer structured, validated AI outputs.
- Keep generated decisions traceable to source documents, calculations, or user inputs.
- Fail safely when model output is missing, malformed, incomplete, or low confidence.
- Require human approval for legal, financial, or irreversible outputs.
- Add fixtures, evaluations, or regression tests for critical AI behavior.

## Review Gate

AI Engineer, QA Lead, and Security Engineer must approve AI changes that affect project decisions, documents, offers, or sensitive data.
