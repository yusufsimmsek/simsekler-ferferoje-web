# Accessibility Standard

## Purpose

ArchiFlow must remain usable for keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion, and varied viewport users.

## Requirements

- Use semantic controls for actions and navigation.
- Preserve visible focus and keyboard reachability.
- Provide labels or accessible names for icon-only controls.
- Do not communicate critical state through color or motion alone.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep contrast and text sizing readable in dense operational layouts.

## Review Gate

Frontend Engineer, UX Researcher, and QA Lead must review accessibility for changed interactive screens.
